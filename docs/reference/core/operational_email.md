# Module: `src.core.operational_email`

Provides the persisted message/template service behind the Operational Email page and its worker schedule queue.

## Template catalog and defaults

`TEMPLATE_DEFINITIONS` declares ten stable template keys, descriptions, fill-in fields, and capability limits:

| Key | Use | Special capability |
|---|---|---|
| `maintenance_notice` | Ticket title, site, planned work, start/end window, risk, disruption, required change department (`IT Network` or `The Network Operations Center`), operational impact, and departmental impact | Maintenance times are date/time values; no Network Orders auto-fill |
| `fiber_maintenance_notice` | Ticket title, fiber work, MOP type (`Demand`, `Standard`, or `Emergency`), and Expected impact | Multi-select Network Orders/Circuit IDs fill A/Z/contact; Fiber recipients are BCC |
| `outage_dispatch` | Request field technician dispatch | — |
| `fiber_outage` | Fiber service interruption by Location/Network Orders/Circuit IDs | Global From is To; matching Fiber contacts go to BCC; no Provider, Estimated restoration, or Requested action field |
| `fiber_outage_onpage` | Fiber outage page to selected ONPAGE departments | ONPAGE contacts are To, Global From is Cc, and Network Orders do not auto-fill BCC |
| `fiber_outage_update` | Progress update to an outage ticket | Requires a sent Fiber Outage or Fiber Outage ONPAGE source; omits ONPAGE To and inherits BCC |
| `fiber_outage_resolution` | Service-restoration notice | Requires a sent Fiber Outage or Fiber Outage ONPAGE source; offers searchable Fiber BCC contacts, omits ONPAGE To, and inherits source BCC |
| `after_hours_onpage` | Urgent after-hours acknowledgement and response | ONPAGE contact multi-select; To recipients only |
| `morning_report` | Latest Global Dashboard shift analysis | Bounded attachments; source refreshed at delivery |
| `maintenance_complete` | Close-out of planned maintenance or Fiber Maintenance | Requires a successfully sent Maintenance Notification or Fiber Maintenance Notification; inherits Fiber closeout details and source BCC |

`ensure_operational_email_templates(session_factory)` seeds missing rows without replacing administrator-edited defaults. Template placeholders use `{{field_name}}`; validation permits only field names declared for the selected template. Maintenance risk is validated against `None`, `Low`, `Medium`, or `High`; disruption is selected from `Non-Disruptive`, `Potentially Disruptive`, and `Disruptive`; planned maintenance start and expected-completion fields are date/time values. Timezone labels are set server-side and hidden from the compose form. Fiber To defaults to the active Global From address; Fiber Outage ONPAGE forces ONPAGE To and Global From Cc. The searchable Fiber-contact BCC picker is available on Fiber Maintenance, Fiber Outage, and Fiber Outage Resolution. BCC defaults and recipients are supported by every template except After-Hours ONPAGE, which is To-only. `update_operational_email_template()` validates subject/body length, placeholders, recipients, and the fixed sender-address defaults, then records an account-audit event.

Administrator-managed signatures have a unique name, plain-text content, active state, and optional default state. At most one active default is selected. Senders can use that default, choose another active signature, or explicitly send without a signature. Selected signature ID/name/content are captured when sending or scheduling, so later edits/deactivation do not alter a scheduled message.

## Message validation and delivery

`normalize_recipient_list()` validates comma/semicolon-separated addresses and removes duplicates. `_normalize_fields()` enforces the selected template's allowed keys, required fields, choices, date/time format, and per-field bounds; multiline values retain their paragraph breaks after line-ending normalization. `get_operational_email_reference_data(include_onpage=False)` returns ordered Fiber contacts and Network Orders by default, and includes ONPAGE addresses only for an ONPAGE template. `_resolve_network_order_contacts()` validates selected record IDs and fills the selected template's location and Network Orders/Circuit IDs fields plus contact text. It uses the selected records' company mappings to match Fiber contacts and add their addresses to BCC, except for Fiber Outage ONPAGE. Network Orders/Circuit IDs are optional for Fiber Outage; Location is operator-entered. Fiber Maintenance Expected impact accepts the legacy `departmental_impact` field for stored/custom templates. `_normalize_attachments()` accepts no more than five files, 5 MiB each and 20 MiB combined; it sanitizes filenames and persists decoded bytes with the message snapshot.

`send_operational_email()` resolves defaults, linked source fields, and the selected signature; validates recipients/fields; persists an `OperationalEmail` row plus attachment bytes; then calls `_attempt_delivery()`. Every attempt creates an immutable `OperationalEmailDelivery` snapshot with the active provider (`smtp` or `gmail`), From address, To/Cc/Bcc envelope, selected signature name, rendered body, and attachment names. BCC is omitted from visible mail headers. The shared mailer handles transport failures; both successful and failed outcomes remain visible in history. Scheduled messages use the profile active at delivery time; completed history retains the provider used for that attempt.

`schedule_operational_email()` accepts one-time timezone-aware ISO timestamps or daily/weekly `America/Chicago` wall-clock schedules. `_next_recurrence()` calculates the next Central occurrence and stores it in UTC. `process_due_operational_emails()` atomically claims due rows, records one delivery per occurrence, advances recurring schedules, and leaves a visible failure state for unsuccessful one-time sends. Morning Report uses the most recent `SystemConfig.rolling_summary` at delivery time and sends it without report-date/window/title/executive-summary wrapping; the selected signature and attachments remain snapshotted. `cancel_scheduled_operational_email()` cancels pending/recurring rows and refuses an in-flight send.

Maintenance completion accepts its legacy `maintenance_notice_id` and current `source_email_id`; both require a successful Maintenance Notification or Fiber Maintenance Notification delivery. Fiber Maintenance closeouts include the source route, circuit IDs, MOP type, and expected impact. Fiber progress/resolution accepts `source_email_id` for a successfully delivered `fiber_outage` or `fiber_outage_onpage` message. Child messages use Global From on To, retain the original BCC list, omit ONPAGE To recipients, and store a `parent_email_id` to the initial ticket.

## Persistence and operational boundaries

Migration `20261117_0016` removes Company placeholders from saved Fiber defaults and moves the change-department body field to Maintenance Notification. Migration `20261117_0017` removes A/Z placeholders from saved Fiber Outage, ONPAGE, follow-up, and resolution defaults. Migration `20261117_0018` adds Operational impact to Maintenance Notification and Fiber details to Maintenance Complete. Migration `20261117_0019` removes Network Orders placeholders from shipped Fiber Outage subjects. These revisions retain other customized text and add no tables or columns. Operational Email uses the plain-text mailer path, preserving template and user-entered line breaks without the automatic `[NOC FUSION]` subject prefix. Fiber contact BCC selection is searchable and limited to Fiber Maintenance, Fiber Outage, and Fiber Outage Resolution; linked messages retain source BCC recipients. Fiber Outage Location is operator-entered and Network Orders/Circuit IDs are optional. Maintenance Complete accepts Fiber Maintenance as a source and includes its circuit and impact details. The Fiber Outage ONPAGE template is seeded when the template catalog is initialized. Earlier revisions create the Operational Email tables, add signature and delivery-provider snapshots, and create the Fiber/ONPAGE contact and Network Order directories. Attachment bytes stay in SQLite so the queue survives restarts and is covered by the encrypted full-database backup. The worker's non-disableable one-minute registry job calls `process_due_operational_emails()`.

All HTTP operations require the `Operational Email` page grant. Shared template defaults and signature management are administrator-only; send, schedule, cancellation, and history do not require separate action grants.
