# Module: `src.core.operational_email`

Provides the persisted message/template service behind the Operational Email page and its worker schedule queue.

## Template catalog and defaults

`TEMPLATE_DEFINITIONS` declares eleven stable template keys, descriptions, fill-in fields, and capability limits:

| Key | Use | Special capability |
|---|---|---|
| `maintenance_notice` | Ticket title, site, planned work, start/end window, risk, disruption, required change department (`IT Network` or `The Network Operations Center`), operational impact, and departmental impact | Maintenance times are date/time values; no Network Orders auto-fill |
| `fiber_maintenance_notice` | Ticket title, fiber work, MOP type (`Demand`, `Standard`, or `Emergency`), and Expected impact | Requires selected Network Orders/Circuits; creates one company-specific message with that company's contacts in To and Global From in Cc |
| `outage_dispatch` | Request field technician dispatch | — |
| `fiber_outage` | Fiber service interruption by Location/Network Orders/Circuit IDs | Global From is To; matching Fiber contacts go to BCC; no Provider, Estimated restoration, or Requested action field |
| `fiber_outage_onpage` | Fiber outage page to selected ONPAGE departments | ONPAGE contacts are To, Global From is Cc, and Network Orders do not auto-fill BCC |
| `fiber_outage_update` | Progress update to an outage ticket | Requires a sent Fiber Outage or Fiber Outage ONPAGE source; omits ONPAGE To and inherits BCC |
| `fiber_outage_resolution` | Service-restoration notice | Requires a sent Fiber Outage or Fiber Outage ONPAGE source; offers searchable Fiber BCC contacts, omits ONPAGE To, and inherits source BCC |
| `after_hours_onpage` | Urgent after-hours acknowledgement and response | ONPAGE contact multi-select; To recipients only |
| `morning_report` | Latest Global Dashboard shift analysis | Bounded attachments; source refreshed at delivery |
| `maintenance_start` | Start notice for a previously announced maintenance window | Requires a successfully sent Maintenance Notification or Fiber Maintenance Notification; inherits source details and recipients |
| `maintenance_complete` | Close-out of planned maintenance or Fiber Maintenance | Requires a successfully sent Maintenance Notification or Fiber Maintenance Notification; inherits Fiber closeout details and source BCC |

`ensure_operational_email_templates(session_factory)` seeds missing rows without replacing administrator-edited defaults. Template placeholders use `{{field_name}}`; validation permits only field names declared for the selected template. Maintenance risk is validated against `None`, `Low`, `Medium`, or `High`; disruption is selected from `Non-Disruptive`, `Potentially Disruptive`, and `Disruptive`; planned maintenance start and expected-completion fields are date/time values rendered in message bodies as `MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM` with a server-set CST/CDT label. Timezone labels are hidden from the compose form. Fiber Outage and linked follow-up To defaults use the active Global From address. Fiber Maintenance To is selected from mapped Fiber contacts for each selected circuit company; Global From is always in Cc, joined with administrator-configured defaults and editable per-message Cc. Maintenance Start and Maintenance Complete default Cc values are administrator-configurable and editable in the composer; linked maintenance compositions combine defaults with source Cc and preserve source BCC. Fiber Outage ONPAGE forces ONPAGE To and Global From Cc. The searchable Fiber-contact BCC picker is available on Fiber Outage and Fiber Outage Resolution. BCC defaults and recipients are supported by every template except After-Hours ONPAGE and Fiber Maintenance; After-Hours ONPAGE is To-only. `update_operational_email_template()` validates subject/body length, placeholders, and recipients, then records an account-audit event.

Administrator-managed signatures have a unique name, plain-text content, active state, and optional default state. At most one active default is selected. Senders can use that default, choose another active signature, or explicitly send without a signature. Selected signature ID/name/content are captured when sending or scheduling, so later edits/deactivation do not alter a scheduled message.

## Message validation and delivery

`normalize_recipient_list()` validates comma/semicolon-separated addresses and removes duplicates. `_normalize_fields()` enforces the selected template's allowed keys, required fields, choices, date/time format, and per-field bounds; multiline values retain their paragraph breaks after line-ending normalization. `get_operational_email_reference_data(include_onpage=False)` returns ordered Fiber contacts and Network Orders by default, and includes ONPAGE addresses only for an ONPAGE template. `_selected_network_order_records()` validates selected IDs; `_resolve_network_order_contacts()` fills aggregate composer fields and adds company-matched contacts to BCC for Fiber Outage and Fiber Outage Resolution, but not ONPAGE. Fiber Maintenance instead groups the selected records by company, requires at least one contact address per company, and prepares a separate To/Cc/body snapshot for each group with only that company's circuits, A/Z locations, and contact details. The entire send or schedule is validated before any group is persisted or delivered. Network Orders/Circuit IDs are optional for Fiber Outage; Location is operator-entered. Fiber Maintenance Expected impact accepts the legacy `departmental_impact` field for stored/custom templates. `_normalize_attachments()` accepts no more than five files, 5 MiB each and 20 MiB combined; it sanitizes filenames and persists decoded bytes with the message snapshot.

`_selected_network_order_records()` follows reciprocal `associated_circuit_network_order_ids` transitively, deduplicates visited rows, safely terminates cycles, and respects per-message associated-ID exclusions. Each connected circuit group is then partitioned by company for Fiber Maintenance delivery.

`send_operational_email()` resolves defaults, linked source fields, and the selected signature; validates recipients/fields; persists an `OperationalEmail` row plus attachment bytes; then calls `_attempt_delivery()`. Fiber Maintenance creates one row/delivery per company and returns an aggregate status with individual results; scheduled Fiber Maintenance creates one persistent schedule per company. Every attempt creates an immutable `OperationalEmailDelivery` snapshot with the active provider (`smtp` or `gmail`), From address, To/Cc/Bcc envelope, selected signature name, rendered body, and attachment names. BCC is omitted from visible mail headers. The shared mailer handles transport failures; both successful and failed outcomes remain visible in history. Scheduled messages use the profile active at delivery time; completed history retains the provider used for that attempt.

`schedule_operational_email()` accepts one-time timezone-aware ISO timestamps or daily/weekly `America/Chicago` wall-clock schedules. `_next_recurrence()` calculates the next Central occurrence and stores it in UTC. `process_due_operational_emails()` atomically claims due rows, records one delivery per occurrence, advances recurring schedules, and leaves a visible failure state for unsuccessful one-time sends. Morning Report uses the most recent `SystemConfig.rolling_summary` at delivery time and sends it without report-date/window/title/executive-summary wrapping; the selected signature and attachments remain snapshotted. `cancel_scheduled_operational_email()` cancels pending/recurring rows and refuses an in-flight send.

Maintenance Start and Maintenance Complete require `source_email_id` for a successful Maintenance Notification or Fiber Maintenance Notification delivery; Maintenance Complete also accepts its legacy `maintenance_notice_id`. Both inherit source operational details and recipients, retain source BCC, and store `parent_email_id`; Fiber Maintenance children also include the source route, circuit IDs, MOP type, and expected impact. Fiber progress/resolution accepts `source_email_id` for a successfully delivered `fiber_outage` or `fiber_outage_onpage` message. Those child messages use Global From on To, retain the original BCC list, omit ONPAGE To recipients, and store a `parent_email_id` to the initial ticket.

## Persistence and operational boundaries

Migration `20261117_0016` removes Company placeholders from saved Fiber defaults and moves the change-department body field to Maintenance Notification. Migration `20261117_0017` removes A/Z placeholders from saved Fiber Outage, ONPAGE, follow-up, and resolution defaults. Migration `20261117_0018` adds Operational impact to Maintenance Notification and Fiber details to Maintenance Complete. Migration `20261117_0019` removes Network Orders placeholders from shipped Fiber Outage subjects. These revisions retain other customized text and add no tables or columns; Maintenance Start is seeded as a missing template row without a schema migration. Operational Email sends plain-text and preformatted HTML alternatives to preserve template and user-entered line breaks; subjects omit the automatic `[NOC FUSION]` prefix. Fiber contact BCC selection is searchable and limited to Fiber Outage and Fiber Outage Resolution; Fiber Maintenance creates company-specific To messages with Global From and configured/per-message Cc. Linked messages retain source BCC recipients. Fiber Outage Location is operator-entered and Network Orders/Circuit IDs are optional. Maintenance Start and Maintenance Complete accept Fiber Maintenance as a source; Start inherits its circuit/impact details, while Complete includes the formatted Fiber details. The Fiber Outage ONPAGE template is also seeded when the catalog is initialized. Earlier revisions create the Operational Email tables, add signature and delivery-provider snapshots, and create the Fiber/ONPAGE contact and Network Order directories. Attachment bytes stay in SQLite so the queue survives restarts and is covered by the encrypted full-database backup. The worker's non-disableable one-minute registry job calls `process_due_operational_emails()`.

All HTTP operations require the `Operational Email` page grant. Shared template defaults and signature management are administrator-only; send, schedule, cancellation, and history do not require separate action grants.
